Aesthetics
==========

My son enjoyed watching Big Bang Theory and Sheldon, long after I'd lost
interest in the series for poking fun at nerds instead of honoring them; but
together we've started watching Stuart Fails To Save The Universe together, we
just watched the 3rd episode, and Stuart's BFA has been mentioned a few times
in the sort of flashbacks you only see early on in a new television series.
Now, the BFA is a Bachelors in Fine Arts, and that hits a bit close to home for
me because I have a Bachelors in Computer Science from an Art school. I took
the Drawing, Photography , Videography , A/V editing, Typography, Color Theory,
Script-Writing, etc classes required for "Interactive Multimedia Design;" and I
had a lot of fun helping friends with their own projects, as a model, test
subject, actor, musician, whatever position they needed. It was a good time;
but lately I've been really feeling my own powerlessness, my own inability to
"Save the Universe" and thinking on my past and how that can inform my future.

This might just be the "Mid-life Crisis" talking, I'm in my mid-40s now, so
I've been looking back and collecting my thoughts and ideas. In college I had a
class called Aesthetics, the philosophy of beauty, taught by Dr Diane Bowser.
