----
title: The Printing Press
---

Somebody recently told me that the LLMs are as transformative, and as widely
criticized as [The Printing Press.][gutenberg]. But what
[Anthropic][alexandria] is doing is the opposite of a Printing
Press,<!--more--> they're using a loophole in copyright law to collect books
and destroy them for "transformative use" in training their models and
preventing model collapse... They trained on [data stolen from pirate
libraries][stolen] like Anna's Archive and LibGen, and when that wasn't enough
they started making their own pirate libraries by scanning and shredding books
&mdash; without giving anything back to the commons they robbed to build themselves
up.

{% include linkpreview.html url="https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/01/27/anthropic-ai-scan-destroy-books/" %}

As a kid who found freedom at the Public Library System, it's devastating to
see "transformative" being applied to destroying books. My very first job was
as a Page at the Carnegie Library, carefully tending to the books and arranging
them by the Library of Congress & Dewie Decimal systems or Alphabetically, making sure
the shelving was non-destructive to the books, so that People could access this
vast world of knowledge easily and publicly available.

The Irony isn't lost on me that [Carnegie was a Robber Baron turned Robin
Hood][carnegie], trying to give back to the communities that provided him so
much material benefit; but this new era of Robber Barons are just destroying
the books, and not even releasing the scans to the public.

They trained their Al on information from the [Commons][common], like [Internet Archive](https://archive.org),
and [pirate libraries][pirates] like Anna's Archive and Libgen, places that Publishers are
attacking for Pirating books instead of paying for expensive licenses.

The real ["Tragedy of the Commons"][commons] isn't that individuals are
trampling the Commons, it's that the Ultra-Wealthy can build their own private
armies &mdash;or lobby the government to let them borrow the government's own
private armies&mdash; to make sure that Individuals cannot access the Commons
we all depend on, while simultaneously taking the Commons for every penny it's
worth.

They took the world's knowledge from these Free Internet Libraries and when
that wasn't enough they are now creating their own private libraries of no
longer existent books. It's piracy of the highest degree, and like the Jack
Sparrow and his Crew, their motto seems to be "Take what you can, give nothing
back!"
