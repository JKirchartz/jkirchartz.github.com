I've been around the block a few times, from Shockwave & SMIL/DHTML to Flash &
Dreamweaver & XHTML to Photoshop, Sketch & HTML4, up to Figma in the era of
HTML5. All of these tools used to design websites and have developers parse
them into coded layouts; and none of these tools made the task any easier for
anyone involved. There's an old proverb that goes "it's a poor craftsman who
blames his tools," but old Craftsmen give the wisdom to "Use the right tool for
the job."

Now the design apps offer no-code and AI-generated solutions, and people are
encouraged to argue with these LLMs to train them in the ways of web
development as-if their jobs depended on it.
