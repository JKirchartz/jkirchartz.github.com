I'm a full-stack developer and creative technologist living in Pittsburgh, Pa.
I'm passionate about permaculture, permacomputing, frameworkless web design
&amp; development, automation, and aesthetics. I graduated from the Art
Institute of Pittsburgh with a Bachelors of Science in Interactive Multimedia
Design. I've spent over 15 years creating solutions for businesses &amp;
agencies such as [Garrison Hughes](http://garrisonhughes.com), [Barkley
REI](http://barkleyREI.com), [Fireman Creative](http://firemancreative.com),
[American Eagle](http://ae.com) and [Google](http://google.com).  For the last
few years I've been using Perl, but I've worked with PHP and
Typescript/Javascript, but I'm also familiar with Python, Coldfusion, Java,
Actionscript, Shell scripting, HTML and CSS, and just about any other language
or framework you need.  My favorite text editor is
[vim](https://tildegit.org/kirch/vim), if you're interested in how my rig is
setup (at the momement) check out my
[dotfiles](https://tildegit.org/kirch/dotfiles), or just ask - by email (see
footer) or [Tumblr](https://stash.jkirchartz.com/ask).

I solve problems online for a living, but also enjoy creating art, music, and
electronics, working on my voice &amp; making weird sounds with my mouth and
anything I can find, practicing various circus skills, feats of
prestidigitation and legerdemain, and a bit of baking & gardening and other

dangerous with a chainsaw; Learning new things is fun.

I love exploring life with my family; my wife, son, and daughter; they make
