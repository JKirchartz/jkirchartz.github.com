### Hello, I'm Joel <span data-IPA="kər·chärtz">Kirchartz</span>. I like to make stuff and share it with people on the web.

I'm a Technologist, a post-post modern rennaisance man, living near Pittsburgh,
Pa.  I'm passionate about art, automation, modular design &amp; web
development, and developing systems. I graduated from the Art Institute of
Pittsburgh with a Bachelors of Science in Interactive Multimedia Design. I've
spent the last 20 years creating solutions at places like [Pair Networks](https://pair.com),
[Garrison Hughes](http://garrisonhughes.com), [American Eagle](https://ae.com), &amp;
[Google](http://google.com).  I've hacked code written in Typescript,
Javascript, Perl, Python, PHP, Coldfusion, Java, Actionscript, C/C++, various
Shells, HTML and CSS, and in general I'm fairly agnostic and just want to use
the best tool for task at hand. My favorite text editor is [vim](https://tildegit.org/kirch/vim),
if you're interested in how my rig is setup (at the momement) check out my
[dotfiles](https://tildegit.org/kirch/dotfiles).

I solve problems online for a living, but also enjoy creating; art, stringed
instruments, instruments with keyboards, and electronics, gardening, working
with my hands, working on my voice &amp; making weird sounds with my mouth and
any adhoc instrument I can find, practicing various circus skills, feats of
prestidigitation and legerdemain, and a bit of baking.  Learning new things is
fun.

I love exploring life with my family; my wife, son, and daughter are the three
most amazing people in my world, I wouldn't be able to continue my work without
their unconditional love and support; but I prefer to protect their privacy online despite my lack of privacy.
