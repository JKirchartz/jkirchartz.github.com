Roog is a small
[rogue-lite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roguelike#Rogue-lites_and_procedural_death_labyrinths)
game demo, where you play as a robot seeking a kitten, but<!--more--> there's a
catch: There's also a puppy somewhere in the labyrinth with you! Don't worry,
nothing bad happens, but the puppy does drewl all over everything. [Can you
find the kitten and escape the maze?](https://tilde.town/~kirch/roog/)

Built with [rot.js](https://ondras.github.io/rot.js/hp/) in the spring of 2026,
based on earlier experiments, It's a roguelike with the text & mechanics of
[robotfindskitten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotfindskitten), a "zen
simulation" game that displays all the non-kitten items on the screen as you
investigate each one to hunt for a kitten. This adaptation adds Rogue
mechanics, A Puppy, and Teleportation for hours of fun.
